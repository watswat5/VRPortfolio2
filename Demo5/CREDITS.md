## Project-created and AI-generated art

Project-authored procedural artwork is generated by scripts under `art/`, including crowd sprites,
poster layouts, the robot, mic, DJ headphones, glasses, spray-can, laser effects, and mouth/LED-face
art. The scripts and their source data are included in the project. The hat conversion pipeline
processes third-party models; those model credits are in [`unity/credits.txt`](unity/credits.txt).
The opponents are built on Microsoft Rocketbox avatars (MIT License; details below).

## Provenance to confirm

The repository does not record an original source, creator, generator, or license for these assets:

- None currently listed.
The baked character speech in `unity/Assets/Characters/*/Lines/` uses the same TTS models and
character-matched voices as live generation. It is generated speech, not an unidentified third-party
voice recording. The project does not keep a per-file manifest of the exact model version, voice ID,
or generation date. The gesture-test and lip-sync-test clips in `unity/Assets/GestureTest/Audio/` and
`unity/Assets/LipSyncTest/Audio/` were generated with the Qwen TTS option documented in
[backend/README.md](backend/README.md) (Qwen3-TTS 1.7B CustomVoice); per-file model versions and voice
configuration are not recorded. `backend/web/clips/demo-verse.wav` is the robot's eSpeak-generated
test verse. These generated clips have identified sources; they are not unidentified third-party
recordings.

These remaining gaps do not establish the license terms for other unverified assets. Confirm their
source and license or replace them before redistribution that requires clear provenance.
