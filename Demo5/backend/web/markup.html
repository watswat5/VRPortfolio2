Phrases and breaks

How to

Each square is a bar on the song's BPM grid; the white fill is its bass, so the breaks stand out. Click a bar, Shift-click (or drag) to select a run, then mark it a phrase (P: a whole turn) or a break (B: nobody starts a turn there). Marking part of a phrase again splits it. Space plays from the selection; ← → move it.
Bars before the first phrase are the intro (the count-in); the song loops from the first phrase to the end of the last marked bar. If the bass hits sit off the bar lines, move the grid a beat with ◀ ▶ and check with the click track.
